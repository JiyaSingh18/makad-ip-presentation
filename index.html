Copyright · Makad

Copyright protection for Makad's creative assets

IP concept — Copyright

What is copyright?

Legal right over original works fixed in a medium — code, art, audio, literature. Protects expression, not ideas.

  • Arises automatically on creation (§13, Copyright Act 1957)
  • Covers: software, artistic works, sound recordings, literary works
  • Author: human creator — AI alone cannot be author (Thaler, 2023)
  • Registration optional but strengthens enforcement (copyright.gov.in)
In Makad

Makad's copyrightable works (author: Jiya Singh)

  • Astromech audio — A-wake-rise, A-affirm, A-laugh… original sound recordings
  • 19 head motions — storyboard v0.2 keyframes, timing, curve codes
  • Face animations — E-neutral, E-laugh, E-gaze… LVGL procedural system
  • Expression composer — firmware + coordination logic
  • Docs & renders — vision, 14 specsheets, concept visuals

Patent · Makad

Could Makad's inventions be patented?

IP concept — Patent

What is a patent?

20-year monopoly on a novel, non-obvious invention with industrial application (§2(1)(j), Patents Act 1970).

  • Must pass: novelty, inventive step, industrial applicability
  • Prior art defeats claims — published papers, existing products
  • Searched via USPTO Patent Public Search, ipindia.gov.in
  • Expensive to file — provisional ~₹1,600+ (India)
In Makad

Patent candidates & my evaluation

  • Candidate 1: 3-axis head (yaw+pitch+roll) in 60 mm neck, ~250 g moving mass on floor droid
  • Candidate 2: Expression composer — semantic intent → 4-channel coordinated output with interruption rules
  • Prior art blocks: desktop robots (Cozmo-class), Plos One 2024 nod kinematics, research platforms
  • My decision: document in RP-01 specs; consider provisional filing only if gate evidence proves novelty post-Dec 2026

Trade Secret · Trademark · Makad

Trade secrets & trademarks in Makad's context

IP concept — Trade Secret

What is a trade secret?

Confidential business information giving competitive advantage — protected by secrecy, not registration. Lost once publicly disclosed.

In Makad
  • Could protect: MJ5/MS7 motion parameters, composer timing tables, tracking thresholds
  • Conflict: architecture + storyboard already on public GitHub — can't claim secrecy on published work
  • Plan: keep numeric tuning private until V1 demo
IP concept — Trademark

What is a trademark?

Registered mark identifying product origin — prevents consumer confusion (Trade Marks Act 1999, ipindia.gov.in).

In Makad
  • Marks: "Makad", "M4K4-D", "M4" wake word — all original, not Disney marks
  • Risk: must never adopt R2-D2, BB-8 or Lucasfilm identifiers
  • Plan: not filing now — no commercial market; Class 9 if productized

Research Paper · Makad

Research papers as prior art in Makad's design

IP concept — Research Paper

What role do research papers play in IP?

Published scholarly work enters the public domain as prior art — limits patent novelty. Can be cited in original work without transferring ownership.

  • Author holds copyright on the paper itself
  • Citing ≠ copying — you reference findings, create original implementation
  • Journal sources: Plos One, IEEE, ACM databases
In Makad

Papers Makad's RP-01 storyboard builds on

  • Plos One 2024 — nod/shake kinematics → informs Makad's head motion timing & amplitude decay
  • HRI / puppetry refs — WALL-E, BB-8 animation research → anticipatory motion in Makad specs
  • My RP-01 storyboard v0.2 — original 19-motion choreography; cites papers, adds my keyframes
  • Prior art from these papers limits patent claims on identical head motion methods

Makad's intent.md: "Original design placeholders… not copied film animation or copyrighted sound samples."

Industry Product · Makad

Anki Vector — industry IP lesson for Makad

IP concept — Industry Product

Why study a commercial product?

A product embodies a bundle of IP — patents, trademarks, trade secrets, copyrighted code. Its lifecycle shows what IP survives company failure.

  • Teaches which IP types matter in practice
  • Shows cloud AI dependency risks
  • Informs strategy for similar projects like Makad
Case study — Anki Vector → Makad

What happened & what Makad learns

  • 2018: Vector — cloud NLU, expressive face, personality (~$250) — same category as Makad
  • 2019: Anki bankrupt → servers offline → robots lost personality features
  • IP sold to Digital Dream Labs — patents, trademarks, codebase transferred
  • Community revival (Wire-Pod) only after DDL open-sourced server code

For Makad: character IP (animations, sounds, behaviour) = survival asset. Cloud NLU = dependency. My expression layer must be independently owned (copyright).

Landmark Case · Makad

Thaler v. Perlmutter — AI authorship & Makad's creative layers

IP concept — Landmark case

Thaler v. Perlmutter (2023)

Dr. Thaler sought US copyright on AI-generated image ("DABUS") with no human input. Refused.

  • D.D.C.: "human creativity is the sine qua non of copyright"
  • USCO AI Guidance (2023): AI-assisted OK; AI-alone not author
  • WIPO IP & AI Toolkit (2024): same principle internationally
Relevance to Makad's AI/ML stack

Which Makad layers are protectable?

  • Cloud STT output — not Makad's copyright; provider's ToS applies
  • Face animations, astromech sounds, 19 motions — human-authored by me → copyrightable
  • Expression composer code — my system design → copyrightable software
  • AI is a pipeline tool in Makad (speech recognition, tracking) — not the author of expressive works

Makad implication: my git history + 31K doc repo proves human authorship — essential for copyright validity.

Critical Analysis · Makad

Makad's IP gaps, ethics & future outlook

IP gaps I found in Makad

  • No copyright registration — automatic protection exists; registration at copyright.gov.in strengthens proof
  • Incomplete OSS audit — LVGL, OpenCV, ESP-IDF, Pi OS licenses not fully tracked
  • Cloud API ToS unverified — demo use of STT output depends on provider terms
  • Public GitHub vs trade secret — storyboard published; motion tuning can stay private until demo
  • Derivative-work line — astromech genre safe; R2 film-accurate sounds/likeness would not be

Ethical / policy (beyond IP)

  • Makad's camera/mic tracking → India DPDP Act 2023 consent requirements
  • EU AI Act 2024 → transparency if productized in EU

Innovation insight & future outlook

  • Core insight: Makad's moat is character IP (expression layer), not AI models — same lesson as Anki Vector
  • Before V1: copyright-register astromech audio + animations; complete license audit
  • If productized: trademark "Makad" Class 9; evaluate patent post-RP-01 evidence
  • Long-term: licensing expression SDK; expandable astromech vocabulary as content IP

My conclusion: For Makad, copyright on the authored expression layer is the only IP worth acting on before Dec 2026 V1.
